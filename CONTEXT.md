# Bitcoin Market Condition Research

This context describes the evidence used to classify Bitcoin's current market environment without predicting or executing trades.

## Language

**Observation**:
A sourced measurement of a market or network metric, including its unit, availability, and timing provenance.
_Avoid_: Raw value, datapoint

**Feature**:
An analysis-ready quantity derived from one or more observations according to a documented transformation.
_Avoid_: Metric, indicator

**Signal**:
An explainable assessment of one market-condition category, including its score or explicit reason for not being scored.
_Avoid_: Trade signal, prediction

**Provisional Score**:
An explainable directional assessment calculated from incomplete but legitimate public observations, with its coverage and source scope stated explicitly.
_Avoid_: Final score, validated score

**Market Condition**:
A classification of Bitcoin's current market environment based on multiple usable signals.
_Avoid_: Forecast, trade recommendation
